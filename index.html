Welcome to HangMan - Word Puzzle (hereinafter referred to as the "Game" or "Application"), created and maintained by BlueAppsBox (also operating under the tag blueappbox, "we", "us", or "our").

2 Information We Collect & Process

HangMan - Word Puzzle is architected as an offline-friendly, privacy-focused educational word puzzle. No account registration, user logins, passwords, emails, or phone numbers are required to play.

The categories of data processed include:

  • Local Gameplay Progression (On-Device Only): Your current unlocked trial (Trials 1 through 300), accumulated stars, category completions, and audio mute preferences are saved strictly on your local device via HTML5 LocalStorage. We do not store or transmit this game progression data to any external corporate servers.
  • Non-Personal Device & Technical Telemetry: Anonymized device characteristics (device brand/model, Android OS version, display resolution, system language, and network state) collected through standard Android operating system interfaces for compatibility.
  • Crash & Performance Logs: Aggregated, non-personally identifiable diagnostic reports used exclusively to detect crashes, fix bugs, and ensure smooth 60 FPS performance on all phone screens.

3 Advertising Partners & Google AdMob

To maintain HangMan - Word Puzzle as a 100% free vocabulary learning game for players around the world, we integrate advertising technology from Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may process non-personal information subject to Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): Used by AdMob for ad frequency capping, fraud mitigation, ad verification, and delivering personalized or non-personalized ads according to your device preferences.
  • Coarse Location (Country / City Level): Inferred from your IP address to deliver regionally relevant ad content.
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Information on ad views and completion of optional player-initiated rewarded video ads (used for unlocking letter hints, restoring +2 extra lives after hanging, or resetting trial mistakes).
Rewarded Ads Transparency: Watching rewarded video ads is entirely optional and initiated solely by the player. You are never forced to watch ads to solve standard trials or advance through the 300 levels.

Learn more about Google’s advertising privacy standards: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4 Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

BlueAppsBox is committed to the digital safety of children and families. HangMan - Word Puzzle is designed as a family-safe vocabulary challenge suitable for players of all ages.

  • We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information (PII) from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your territory).
  • Our ad integration follows Google Play’s Families Policy and Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) guidelines.
  • If a parent or legal guardian discovers that their child has inadvertently transmitted personal data through third-party ad networks without consent, please contact us immediately at blueappsmanager@gmail.com, and we will take immediate corrective action.

5 User Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the European Economic Area under GDPR and California under CCPA/CPRA), you retain fundamental rights regarding your digital data:

  • Right to Erase All Game Data: You can completely purge all unlocked chapters, saved stars, and preferences anytime in one step: Android Settings → Apps → HangMan - Word Puzzle → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Ad Personalization: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID directly from your Android system settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete Advertising ID.
  • Privacy Inquiries: For any privacy-related queries or requests, contact our dedicated support team directly via email at blueappsmanager@gmail.com.
